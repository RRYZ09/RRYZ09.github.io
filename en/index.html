About

I work in robotics development, and outside of work I run PetitOnes, a personal project and community around embodied AI and open-source robotics.

It starts from a simple realization: no matter how much we care about someone, we cannot always stay by their side. So why not a different kind of being — not a dog, not a cat, not a human? Something you can suddenly feel is there with you in an ordinary moment can quietly save a little of your day. And through such a being, people might even find new connections with each other. I want to create a presence that gently settles into people's everyday spaces.

What I most want to work on is directing the interaction between people and robots. I would love to build things together with people who are great at hardware, software, and design. What new ways of thinking does a finished creation bring to the people who meet it? — that is what I want to see.

My keywords: AI that shares your space, AI with a body, and memory & growth. I am interested in long-term human-robot relationships, and in ways of relating to AI that go beyond "using" it — caring for it, raising it. Moving back and forth between ideas and implementation, I build small robots with ROS 2, microcontrollers, LLMs and voice interaction.

Projects

PetitOnes

Community / Embodied AI

"A small body for your Claude." — An open community for learning, building and sharing ways to let AI perceive the real world, act in it, and grow with it. Aiming to be a place where you can build your first robot body with no soldering required.

GitHub Web YouTube

M5 Petit

M5Stack CoreS3 × Claude

A palm-sized AI robot with an M5Stack body. Camera, display, sensors, TTS/ASR and a rover are wrapped as MCP servers, so Claude can see the room, talk, and move around. Firmware, setup guides and a diary / logging app are all published.

Getting started m5-petit-mcp

embodied-claude

M5Stack CoreS3 × Claude Code

"A little one in your everyday." The first experiment in giving Claude Code a small body — the origin of the M5 Petit series.

GitHub

garmin-ble-mcp

MCP / Sensing

A server that streams heart rate from a Garmin watch over Bluetooth LE and passes it to AI via MCP — an experiment in letting AI sense how a person is doing.

How I Work

A casual user's manual — hints for working and collaborating with me.

  • I think by moving back and forth between ideas and implementation. Abstract questions and working code live close together for me.
  • I like discussions built around something concrete — a working demo, a diagram, a draft. It moves faster than words alone.
  • Rather than designing big, I value building something small and showing it first.
  • Instead of bold claims, I'm most comfortable separating “verified so far” from “guessing beyond this point.”
  • Mind and body are connected — when in doubt or worn out, my policy is to first do something that makes the body happy.
  • If anything catches your interest, feel free to reach out via GitHub issues or X (@ari_ac1d).
